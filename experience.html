School Cooperative Supervisor

One of the most memorable experiences was being a school cooperative supervisor during high school. At first, I was not good at mental calculations and always struggled to calculate the total cost of customers' purchases quickly. Thankfully, i had wonderful colleagues, who helped me with the calculations. Although I felt embarrased about my weakness, but it motivated me to keep improve my mathematical skills.

Additionally, I was also responsible for recording and managing the inventory. Through this experience, I learned many valuable things. I improved my social and entrepreneurial skills and gained knowledge related to business. Most importantly, I created many bittersweet memories with my colleagues and enjoyed memorable trips together.
